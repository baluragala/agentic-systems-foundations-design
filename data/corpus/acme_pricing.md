# Acme Cloud Pricing

Acme Cloud offers three subscription tiers: **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Enterprise**.

## Starter

The Starter plan costs **$29 per month** and includes 3 seats, 50 GB of object
storage, and 100,000 API calls per month. Starter is billed monthly and can be
cancelled at any time. It does not include priority support.

## Growth

The Growth plan costs **$199 per month** and includes 15 seats, 1 TB of object
storage, and 5,000,000 API calls per month. Growth adds email support with a
next-business-day response target and access to the analytics dashboard.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated per contract. It includes unlimited
seats, dedicated storage, a 99.99% uptime SLA, single sign-on (SSO), and a named
technical account manager. Enterprise customers are billed annually.

## Overage and Add-ons

API calls beyond the plan limit are billed at **$0.50 per 1,000 calls**.
Additional object storage can be purchased at **$0.02 per GB per month**. All
prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable taxes.

## Free Trial

New accounts receive a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is
required to start the trial. At the end of the trial the account reverts to the
Starter plan unless a paid plan is selected.
