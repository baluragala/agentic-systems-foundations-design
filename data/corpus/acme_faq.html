Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my data residency region later?

No. Data residency is chosen at account creation and is permanent. Moving data to a different region requires a paid data migration engagement handled by the Acme professional services team.

Do you offer a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Paying annually gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing on the Starter and Growth plans. Enterprise contracts are billed annually by default.

What happens if I exceed my API rate limit?

The API returns HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header. Your requests are not charged when they are rejected for rate limiting, but repeated violations may trigger a temporary block.

Is there a data retention policy?

Deleted objects are retained in a recoverable state for 30 days, after which they are permanently purged. Backups are kept for 90 days.

Which programming languages have official SDKs?

Acme provides official SDKs for Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Go, and Java. Community SDKs exist for Ruby and PHP but are not officially supported.
